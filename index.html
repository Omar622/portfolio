How the tenant migration worked
Problem
Two write flows ran side by side for years. Their data drifted apart, the old flow caused production issues, and reads had to pick a store for each tenant.
Decision
Dashboards in an observability product don't need second-by-second data. So for each switch we paused the consumers, moved the traffic to the new flow, and resumed. Writes waited in the queue for up to 30 minutes. Nothing was lost.
Rollout
Non-tenant production traffic first, then a week of monitoring, then one tenant a day, about 5 in total. We watched throughput and metrics after each switch. The old flow stayed up until every tenant was stable, and rollback was moving the tenant back.
Result
The team spent about 6 months getting the code ready. I wrote most of the implementation. One write flow: no data mismatches, no per-tenant read logic.
